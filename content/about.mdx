## The short version

I'm a final-year Computer Engineering student at General Sir John Kotelawala Defence University in Sri Lanka, and a Software Engineering Intern at WSO2. At WSO2 I build the self-service identity components in ThunderID: the profile and account screens that developers drop into their React, Next.js, Vue, Nuxt or Express apps instead of building them from scratch.

I like layers other people build on. A good SDK component disappears into someone else's product. It inherits their brand, works with their keyboard users and screen readers, and doesn't make them think about the auth flow under it.

Outside identity, I've worked closer to the metal. I built an ESP32-S3 robot whose firmware isn't allowed to reverse, and ML models for power-trace detection and EV battery prediction.

## How I work

- **Contracts first.** I settle the props, tokens and events before the pixels. Theming is an API, so I design it like one.
- **Make it testable by touching it.** That's why this site has demos instead of adjectives.
- **Write down what broke.** Every case study here has a "what went wrong" section, because that's where I learned the most.
- **Small, reviewable PRs.** Monorepos punish big-bang changes.
